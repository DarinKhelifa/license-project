About ORELAX
=============

Who we are
----------
ORELAX is a resident-centered community management platform designed to simplify daily life in apartment complexes, gated communities and residential buildings. Built by a team with deep experience in property operations, resident services, and secure software development, ORELAX provides modern tools for residents, property managers, and security teams to communicate, book facilities, report issues, and stay informed.

Our mission
-----------
To make residential living safer, more convenient, and more connected by delivering an easy, secure, and reliable digital experience for residents and staff.

What ORELAX does
----------------
- Centralized resident directory and contact management
- Facility and amenity booking (gyms, meeting rooms, pools, etc.)
- Event announcements and community news
- Incident reporting and secure communications with building security
- Visitor management and QR-enabled guest access
- Real-time notifications and alerts

Who benefits
------------
- Residents: faster support, simple booking, and direct communications with management.
- Property managers: streamlined operations, booking oversight, and reporting tools.
- Security teams: incident tracking, secure contact channels, and visitor access control.

Core values
-----------
- Security: We protect resident data and communications with industry best practices.
- Simplicity: Intuitive design so everyone — regardless of tech experience — can use ORELAX.
- Reliability: Robust infrastructure and responsive support when you need it.
- Community: We build features that strengthen resident engagement.

Getting started (for residents)
--------------------------------
1. Create an account using the invitation or sign-up link provided by your building.
2. Complete your profile and verify your apartment/identity per community rules.
3. Explore the `Bookings` tab to reserve amenities.
4. Use `Report` or `Support` to raise issues or contact management.
5. Keep notifications enabled to receive important alerts.

Learn more
----------
If you’d like us to integrate features for your building or need help onboarding, contact our team via the Help Center.

Thank you for choosing ORELAX — we’re here to make community living easier and safer.
