Help Center — ORELAX
=====================

Overview
--------
This Help Center provides quick guidance to get the most out of ORELAX, answers to common questions, and contact channels for support and security.

Quick Start — Common Tasks
--------------------------
- Create or update your resident profile: Open the `Profile` screen and follow the prompts.
- Book an amenity: Go to `Bookings` → select amenity → pick a time slot → confirm.
- Report an issue: Use `Report` or `Support` to submit a description, attach photos, and choose a category (maintenance, security, general).
- View community announcements: Check the `News` or `Announcements` section on the home screen.
- Manage visitors: Generate a QR guest pass from `Visitors` and share with your guest.

FAQ
---
Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the `Forgot Password` link on the sign-in screen. Follow the emailed instructions.

Q: Who can see my reports?
A: Reports are visible to authorized building staff; sensitive security reports are routed to the security team.

Q: Can I invite family members or guests?
A: Yes — use `Visitors` to generate guest passes or add dependent accounts if your building supports it.

Security & Privacy
------------------
Your privacy and safety are our top priorities. If you encounter suspicious activity or need to report an urgent security incident, contact the security team immediately.

Support Guidelines
------------------
- For non-urgent maintenance requests, include photos and the apartment number.
- For urgent safety or security incidents, call your building’s on-site security first (if applicable), then email the Security Team.
- Expect a reply within 24–48 business hours for standard requests. Security incidents are prioritized.
